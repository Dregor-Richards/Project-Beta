Controls

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move, I to open/close your inventory, G to open/close the glossary, E or Enter to confirm, and F to replay or quick start. Press C to center the camera on your character, right-click and drag to pan the view, and -/= or the mouse scroll to zoom in/out.

Instructions

Using the controls, move your avatar (Green) throughout the dungeon. Your goal is to eliminate all enemies (Red) by moving onto their space. Each enemy has their own movement and capabilities, so watch out! Once you defeat all enemies, move to the door (White) to reach the next level.
