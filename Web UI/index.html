Shiervale Dungeons

Control Configuration

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move.

Instructions

Using the controls, move your avatar (Green) throughout the dungeon. Your goal is to eliminate all enemies (Red) by moving onto their space. Each enemy has their own movement and capabilities, so watch out! Once you defeat all enemies, move to the door (White) to reach the next level.

Venture into dungeons galore, in search of lost secrets and ancient treasures!
